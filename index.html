What we do

One robot, one job, one monthly bill.

We are not a robot manufacturer. We pick the right machine for your task from the makers we trust, then make it work on your floor.

Rent

Task-specific robots by the week, month or job. Cleaning, moving, delivering, inspecting. The robot is ours; the work is yours.

Run

We survey the site, install, train your team, monitor remotely and maintain. If a unit fails, we swap it. You never call a hotline in another time zone.

Match

Independent advice on which robot fits which job, and honest answers when none does yet. We test the machines so you do not have to.

How it works

From first call to a working robot in three steps.

1

Tell us the task

A 20-minute call and a short site visit. We look at the floor, the hours, the people and the mess. If a robot is not the answer, we say so.

2

Run a paid pilot

Four weeks, one robot, one task, a fixed fee. We install it, train your lead and measure hours saved and quality delivered.

3

Scale or stop

Keep it on a monthly plan, add units, switch to a better model when one appears, or hand it back. No lock-in either way.

Why Humanist

Machines for the work. People for the judgement.

The name is the promise. We put robots to work for people, not instead of them. The jobs we automate are the ones that wear bodies down and minds out: the night-shift scrub, the thousandth pallet, the patrol at three in the morning.

Every Humanist robot runs under a named person on your team. They decide what it does, when it stops and whether it stays. We build the tools; your people stay in charge.

We are based in Amsterdam and work across the Netherlands.

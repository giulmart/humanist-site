How it works

From first call to a working humanoid in three steps.

Step 01

Tell us the task

A 20-minute call and a site visit. We look at the floor, the hours, the people and the work. If a humanoid is not the answer yet, we say so.

Step 02

Run a paid pilot

One unit, one task, a fixed fee. We install it, train your lead, and measure hours delivered and quality against your baseline.

Step 03

Scale or stop

Keep it on a monthly plan, add units, move to a newer platform when one earns it, or hand it back. No lock-in either way.

Why Humanist

Machines for the work. People for the judgement.

The name is the promise. We put humanoids to work for people, not instead of them. The jobs we automate are the ones that wear bodies down and minds out: the night shift, the thousandth tote, the press that never stops.

Every Humanist unit runs under a named person on your team. They decide what it does, when it stops and whether it stays. We supply the machine; your people stay in charge.
